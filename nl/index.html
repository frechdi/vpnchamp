Veelgestelde Vragen

What is a VPN and why do I need one?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) encrypts your internet connection and routes it through a secure server, protecting your online privacy, securing your data on public Wi-Fi, and allowing you to access content as if you were in a different location.

Are VPNs legal?

Yes, VPNs are legal in most countries. However, their usage is restricted or banned in a few countries. Always check your local regulations. VPNs are commonly used for legitimate purposes like protecting personal data and accessing streaming services while traveling.

How do I choose the right VPN for my needs?

Consider your primary use case (streaming, privacy, gaming), required features (kill switch, no-logs), server locations, connection speed, and customer support. Our comparison tool and category-specific guides help you find the perfect VPN.

What is the best VPN?

The "best" VPN depends on your needs. For streaming, try ExpressVPN or NordVPN. For privacy-focused users, Mullvad or ProtonVPN are excellent. For budget-conscious users, Surfshark or PIA offer great value. Check our detailed reviews to compare.

Can I use a free VPN?

While free VPNs exist, they often have limitations: slower speeds, fewer servers, data limits, ads, and privacy concerns. Premium VPNs offer better security, faster speeds, and broader server networks. Most offer money-back guarantees.
