Best VPN for China - Frequently Asked Questions

Are VPNs legal in China?

VPNs are technically restricted in China. Using them may violate local law, though tourists and expatriates have generally used them without major issues.

What is obfuscation?

Obfuscation technology disguises VPN traffic as regular internet traffic, making it harder for the Great Firewall to detect and block.

Which VPNs most reliably work in China?

ExpressVPN and Surfshark are known for reliable performance in China due to continuous server updates and obfuscation protocols.

Should I install the VPN before arriving in China?

Yes, strongly recommended. Many VPN websites are blocked in China, making it difficult to download or access the app once there.
