Best VPN for Maximum Privacy - Frequently Asked Questions

What is a no-logs policy?

A no-logs policy means the VPN provider doesn't record your browsing activity, IP address, or connection data. This is verified through independent audits.

Which VPNs have been independently audited?

ProtonVPN, Mullvad, NordVPN, and ExpressVPN have undergone independent security audits confirming their privacy practices.

Is 256-bit encryption really necessary?

For most users, 256-bit AES encryption (military-grade) is more than sufficient. It would take thousands of years to crack with current technology.

Can a VPN prevent ISP tracking?

Yes, a VPN encrypts traffic between your device and the VPN server, preventing your ISP from seeing your browsing activity.
