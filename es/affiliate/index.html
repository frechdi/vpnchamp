Transparencia sobre cómo generamos ingresos

How We Earn Money

VPNChamp is a free resource supported by affiliate commissions. When you click a link on our site and purchase a VPN subscription, we may receive a commission from the VPN provider. This comes at no extra cost to you.

Does This Affect Our Reviews?

No. Our rankings and reviews are based entirely on objective testing and analysis. We evaluate every VPN on speed, security, privacy, features, and value — not on commission rates. A provider paying us more does not result in a higher ranking.

Which Providers Do We Work With?

We have affiliate relationships with most major VPN providers including NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Surfshark, CyberGhost, ProtonVPN, and others. If a provider is featured on our site, assume we may earn a commission if you sign up through our link.

Our Promise

  • We will never recommend a VPN we wouldn't use ourselves
  • Rankings reflect real performance data, not business relationships
  • We disclose affiliate links clearly throughout the site
  • We regularly re-test VPNs to ensure our recommendations stay current
